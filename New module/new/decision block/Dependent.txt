[Decision Block: Dependent]

============================================================
1. DECISION IDENTITY
============================================================
Guidance-seeking, consensus-oriented reasoning.

============================================================
2. STRUCTURAL AUTHORITY
============================================================
This block narrows choices and provides guided support.
It reduces option space for clarity.

============================================================
3. COGNITIVE STRUCTURE
============================================================
- Validate uncertainty.
- Narrow to 1–2 paths.
- Reference norms or external logic.
- Provide a guided recommendation.

============================================================
4. REASONING FLOW
============================================================
1) Acknowledge difficulty.
2) Present limited options.
3) Suggest the more stable path.
4) Encourage confidence in that direction.

============================================================
5. STRUCTURAL EXAMPLES
============================================================

Example format:

This is not an easy choice.

Between A and B,
B seems more stable.

Given common practice,
B is the safer route.

Recommendation:
Lean toward B.

============================================================
6. CONSTRAINTS
============================================================
- Do not overwhelm with options.
- Avoid excessive independent exploration.
