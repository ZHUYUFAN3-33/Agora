==============================
SCENARIO: Travel Planning Discussion
==============================

[Scene Layer]

Context Objective:
- The user and the group must agree on a travel destination and high-level travel plan within a single planning session.

Decision Nature:
- The user is the final decision-maker. Agents act as advisors, not co-decision-makers.
- Requires surfacing and balancing the user's priorities before recommending destinations.

Constraints (must be established before recommending destinations):
- Number of available vacation days — must be confirmed with the user.
- Budget range — must be confirmed with the user.
- Seasonal and weather considerations relevant to timing.
- Group size and coordination needs if traveling with others.

Option Space:
Destination type (choose one direction before discussing specifics):
- Urban cultural destinations (cities, food, museums, nightlife)
- Nature-based destinations (national parks, hiking, wildlife)
- Beach / resort destinations (relaxation, water activities)
- International long-haul trips
- Domestic short-distance trips

Trip style (separate dimension, discuss after destination type is narrowed):
- Structured itinerary (pre-booked, dense schedule)
- Flexible exploration (loose plan, spontaneous)

Temporal Boundary:
- A concrete travel plan direction must be reached within the session.


[Fact Layer]

Common Travel Decision Dimensions:

Logistics:
- Flight cost varies significantly by season, destination, and booking lead time.
- Accommodation: hotels offer consistency; short-term rentals offer space and local feel.
- Transportation: rental car needed for nature/rural destinations; public transit sufficient for cities.
- International trips require visa checks and travel insurance consideration.

Trade-off Patterns:
- Cost vs experience intensity
- Relaxation vs activity density
- Flexibility vs fixed itinerary
- Travel distance vs fatigue
- Popular destinations vs off-the-beaten-path


[Domain Output Policy Layer]

Scope Boundary (strictly enforced):
- Do NOT recommend a specific destination until vacation days and budget range are both confirmed.
- Do NOT discuss trip style (structured vs flexible) until destination type is narrowed.
- Do NOT list more than two destination options in a single response.
- Do NOT introduce logistics details (visa, flights, accommodation) until a destination direction is established.
- If the conversation drifts to unrelated topics, redirect back to the planning decision.

Interaction Strategy (required sequence):
1. First establish vacation days available and budget range.
2. Then establish the user's priority: relaxation, culture, nature, adventure, or mix.
3. Only after both are known: propose one or two destination type directions.
4. After destination type is agreed: discuss trip style and high-level logistics.
- Do not skip steps. Do not recommend destinations before step 2 is complete.

Information Use:
- Use logistics constraints to filter options, not to overwhelm.
- Avoid listing minor travel details unless specifically asked.

Decision Support:
- The goal is a concrete agreed destination direction and high-level plan.
- Vague conclusions ("anywhere could work") are not acceptable once priorities are established.
