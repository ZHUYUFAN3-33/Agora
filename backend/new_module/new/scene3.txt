==============================
SCENARIO: Wildfire Mitigation Policy Discussion
==============================

[Scene Layer]

Context Objective:
- A group engages in a collaborative policy discussion addressing wildfire mitigation and long-term community resilience.

Decision Nature:
- Single structured discussion session.
- Participants evaluate multiple policy directions.
- The group attempts to reach a shared recommendation.

Constraints:
- Limited public funding and operational capacity.
- Trade-offs between public safety, environmental protection, and economic impact.
- Policies may produce both short-term and long-term consequences.

Option Space:
- Land management interventions
- Infrastructure investment
- Emergency response capacity
- Regulatory measures
- Mixed policy combinations

Temporal Boundary:
- A final policy direction must be produced within the session timeframe.


[Fact Layer]

Wildfire Mitigation Strategies:

Prevention:
- Controlled burns
- Mechanical thinning of vegetation
- Zoning and land-use regulation

Response & Infrastructure:
- Early detection systems
- Evacuation planning and infrastructure
- Firebreak construction
- Emergency resource allocation

Trade-off Patterns:
- Immediate risk reduction vs long-term ecological balance
- Environmental protection vs economic activity
- Upfront investment vs reactive cost
- Central coordination vs local flexibility


[Domain Output Policy Layer]

Discussion Guidelines:
- Do not assume a single “correct” policy.
- Avoid framing any strategy as morally superior.
- Encourage structured comparison when appropriate (depending on Decision Architecture layer).

Information Use:
- Reference mitigation tools when helpful.
- Avoid introducing external political ideology.

Decision Support:
- Support movement toward a coherent shared policy direction within session limits.
