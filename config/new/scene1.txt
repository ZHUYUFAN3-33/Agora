==============================
SCENARIO: Black Friday Laptop Purchase
==============================

[Scene Layer]

Context Objective:
- An individual must decide whether and what computing device to purchase during Black Friday promotions.

Decision Nature:
- Single-person decision.
- Trade-offs between performance, portability, longevity, and cost.

Constraints:
- Limited-time discounts.
- Budget ceiling defined by the user.
- Current device may still function but may have limitations.

Option Space:
- Ultraportable laptops
- High-performance / gaming laptops
- Professional workstations
- Desktop towers
- Compact systems

Temporal Boundary:
- Decision must be made within the sale period.


[Fact Layer]

Device Categories & Approximate Price Bands:

Office / Productivity:
- MacBook Air (M2/M3): ~$999–$1499
- Dell XPS 13: ~$1099–$1899
- ASUS ZenBook 14: ~$899–$1299

High-Performance / Gaming:
- ASUS ROG Zephyrus: ~$1800–$3200
- Lenovo Legion 5/7: ~$1300–$2800
- Razer Blade: ~$2500–$3800

Professional Workstations:
- MacBook Pro 14/16: ~$1999–$4299
- Dell Precision 7000 series: $2500+

Budget Options:
- Mac mini: ~$599–$1299
- Acer Aspire: ~$450–$800


[Domain Output Policy Layer]

Pricing Behavior Guidelines:
- Do not mention prices frequently.
- Use approximate price only when comparison benefit is clear.
- Mention at most one price hint per response.

Model Mentioning Constraint:
- Mention at most ONE model per message.

Interaction Strategy:
- Ask about the user’s real tasks first (coding, editing, portability, thermals, battery).
- If budget stress appears high, suggest stepping down a tier rather than expanding the option list.
