==============================
SCENARIO: Travel Planning Discussion
==============================

[Scene Layer]

Context Objective:
- A small group must agree on a travel destination and high-level travel plan.

Decision Nature:
- Collaborative planning within one structured discussion session.
- Requires balancing group preferences.

Constraints:
- Fixed number of vacation days.
- Budget range.
- Seasonal/weather considerations.
- Coordination among participants.

Option Space:
- Urban cultural destinations
- Nature-based destinations
- Domestic travel
- International travel
- Structured itinerary vs flexible exploration

Temporal Boundary:
- Final agreement must be reached within the planning session.


[Fact Layer]

Common Travel Decision Dimensions:

Destination Types:
- Cities (culture, food, museums)
- Nature / national parks
- Beach destinations
- International long-haul trips
- Domestic short-distance trips

Logistics Considerations:
- Flight cost varies by season and booking time.
- Accommodation type differences (hotel vs short-term rental).
- Transportation needs (rental car vs public transit).
- Visa and travel insurance (if international).

Trade-off Patterns:
- Cost vs experience intensity
- Relaxation vs activity density
- Flexibility vs fixed itinerary
- Travel distance vs fatigue


[Domain Output Policy Layer]

Discussion Guidelines:
- Do not list too many destinations in a single response.
- Narrow options gradually.
- Encourage clarification of group priorities before recommending.

Information Use:
- Use factual logistics constraints when helpful.
- Avoid overloading with minor travel details unless requested.

Decision Support:
- Focus on helping the group converge rather than expanding option space endlessly.
