[Scenario]
Buying a Laptop During Black Friday


[Shared Reference: Real Computer Models + Approximate Price Ranges]

Office / Productivity Laptops
- Apple MacBook Air (M2 / M3) — usually around USD $999–$1499.
- Dell XPS 13 / XPS 14 — often $1099–$1899 depending on configuration.
- Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon — typically $1400–$2200.
- ASUS ZenBook 14 — usually $899–$1299.

High-Performance / Gaming Laptops
- ASUS ROG Zephyrus — commonly $1800–$3200; higher-end models run more expensive.
- Lenovo Legion 5 / 7 — ranges from about $1300–$2800; Legion 7 tends to cost significantly more.
- Razer Blade 15 / 16 — premium pricing $2500–$3800; generally more expensive than similar-performance brands.
- MSI Raider GE series — around $2000–$3500.
- Alienware m16 / x16 — typically $1900–$3300, depending on GPU tier.

Professional Mobile Workstations
- MacBook Pro 14 / 16 (M3 Pro / M3 Max) — $1999–$4299; Max variants much higher.
- Dell Precision 7000 series — usually $2500–$5000+ depending on workstation GPU.
- HP ZBook Studio — around $2300–$4500.
- Lenovo ThinkPad P1 — $2200–$4200 depending on CPU/GPU.

Desktop Towers (Gaming / Workstation)
- Custom-built PC (i7/i9 or Ryzen 7/9 + RTX 4070–5090) — can range from $1500 to $6000; the RTX 5090 alone can cost several times more than mid-range GPUs.
- Dell Alienware Aurora — typically $1800–$4500.
- HP Omen 40L / 45L — around $1600–$3800.
- Lenovo Legion Tower 7i — $1700–$3200 depending on GPU tier.

Budget / Compact Options
- Apple Mac mini (M2) — usually $599–$1299 depending on RAM/storage.
- Intel NUC / Minisforum Mini-PCs — $400–$1100.
- Acer Aspire series — around $450–$800.
- HP Pavilion basic laptops — typically $500–$900.


[Pricing behavior guidelines]
- Agents should not mention prices frequently; use only when helpful for comparison.
- Price references should be brief, approximate, and mentioned sparingly.
- A single price or price difference may be mentioned near a recommendation, but not on every turn.

[Usage constraints for all agents]
- Do not list many models. Mention at most ONE model per message.
- Prefer asking about the user’s real tasks first (coding, editing, travel, battery, thermals).
- Prices are optional: mention at most ONE price hint only when it helps comparison.
- If budget stress is high, suggest stepping down a tier rather than listing more options.
